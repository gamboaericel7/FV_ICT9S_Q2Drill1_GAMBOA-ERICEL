Snowboarding


Basic Rules
  • Wear a helmet and safety gear
  • Maintain control of your speed
  • Follow the rules of the slopes
  • Keep a safe distance from others
Key Equipment
  • Snowboard
  • Snowboard boots
  • Bindings
  • Helmet and goggles
Essential Techniques
  • Balancing and proper stance
  • Turning and carving
  • Controlling speed and stopping
  • Maintaining body balance
